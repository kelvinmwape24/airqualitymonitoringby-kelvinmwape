Understanding Air Quality & Thermal Comfort in Zambia

Temperature and humidity directly affect health, productivity, and well-being. Our network helps you stay informed.

Temperature-Humidity Index (THI)

THI > 75 = Discomfort, > 80 = Severe risk. Monitor to avoid heat exhaustion.

Health Effects

High heat + humidity increases respiratory issues, dehydration, and cardiovascular strain. Children and elderly most vulnerable.

Protective Actions

Drink water, use fans, limit outdoor work during peak sun (11am-3pm), wear light clothes.

Zambia’s Climate Challenge

Rapid urbanization creates heat islands. Green spaces and smart monitoring reduce risks.

How This Project Helps

Provides real-time local data, empowers communities, and supports government policy for healthier cities.

Community Engagement

Anyone can submit readings; schools, health posts, and local leaders can use dashboards for early warnings.

For Every Zambian: Why It Matters

From Lusaka's high-density compounds to Copperbelt mining towns, accurate environmental data saves lives. This platform bridges the gap between citizens and environmental intelligence — free, accessible, and built for Zambia.

About the Project

Project: Design and Implementation of an Environmental Comfort and Air Quality Indicator Network for Urban Areas in Zambia

Researcher: Mwape Kelvin (202203897) | Supervisor: Mrs. Mulwanda

Institution: Mulungushi University, School of Engineering and Technology

Technology: DHT22 sensors, ESP32/Arduino, Node.js/SQLite, modern web dashboard. The system provides real-time heat stress alerts and supports SDG 3, 11, and 13.

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How accurate is DHT22 data?

±0.5°C temperature, ±2% humidity — reliable for urban monitoring.

❓ How can I contribute?

Use the "Add Reading" page with your sensor data or contact us for node installation.

❓ Is this available across Zambia?

We aim to expand to all 10 provinces. Communities can adopt low-cost nodes.

❓ What does THI mean?

Temperature-Humidity Index; above 75 indicates discomfort, above 80 is dangerous.

❓ Can schools/health centers use this?

Absolutely! The dashboard is free and can be displayed in public spaces for awareness.
